About Us

Welcome to Sikri Junior Academy. At Sikri Junior Academy, we cultivate a nurturing environment where young minds are empowered to excel academically, socially, and emotionally. Our mission is to deliver a comprehensive education that fosters academic excellence, instills discipline, and encourages lifelong learning. Founded in 2013, we have a proud tradition of academic achievement and employ innovative teaching strategies designed to meet the unique needs of each student.

Why Choose Sikri Junior Academy

Experienced Educators

Our dedicated team of qualified teachers is passionate about providing high-quality education tailored to each child's needs.

Holistic Learning Environment

We offer a balanced curriculum that combines academic excellence with extracurricular activities, ensuring well-rounded development.

Safe and Nurturing Atmosphere

The safety and well-being of our students are our top priorities. We provide a secure and supportive environment where children thrive.

Modern Facilities

Our state-of-the-art classrooms, library, and play areas are designed to enhance learning and creativity.

Affordable Quality Education

We believe every child deserves access to quality education at an affordable cost, making us the preferred choice for parents in our community.

Community Involvement

We value our partnership with the community, involving parents and local organizations in the academic and extracurricular development of our students.

  • Copy of Immunization Certificate – Reception to Preschool only
  • Copy of Child’s Birth Certificate
  • Copy of parent’s/guardian’s valid Passport and visa if not a Kenyan citizen.
  • Copy of Pupil’s valid Pass/Visa if not a Kenyan citizen.
  • Passport Size Photographs (2 for student and 1 for each parent/guardian).
  • Copy of Mother’s ID Card/Passport and 1 Passport Size Photo
  • Copy of Father’s ID Card/Passport and 1 Passport Size Photo
  • Certified copy of Last Report from Previous School.
  • Certified copy of student’s Leaving Certificate/Clearance Letter.
  • Nemis Number and Assessment Number (CBC Curriculum)
  • Certified copy of KPSEA results (Grade 7 & 8 CBC Curriculum)
